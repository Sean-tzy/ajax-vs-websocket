      What should we notice?

      Both methods work. The big difference is how they find out about news.

      🔁AJAX Polling

      Repeated HTTP checks. Easy to understand, but extra requests can happen when nothing has changed.

      ⚡WebSockets

      One ongoing, two-way connection. Great when the app needs frequent, immediate updates.

      🧠Remember!

      Polling = keep asking.
      WebSockets = keep talking.
